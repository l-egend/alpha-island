# 2.4G 设备电量 / 2.4G device batteries

将受支持接收器、标准 HID 和手柄电量接入 Alpha Island 的电池卡片。读数依赖型号与协议，未上报或已过期的电量会显示未知。

安装包、版本、要求和使用方式以 [中文说明](../examples/DeviceBatteryPlugin/README.md) 和 [插件目录](../README.md) 为准。

Feed supported receiver, standard HID and controller batteries into the Alpha Island battery card. Support depends on the model and protocol; missing or stale readings remain unknown.

See the [English instructions](../examples/DeviceBatteryPlugin/README.en.md) and [plugin catalog](../README.en.md) for packages, versions, requirements and usage.
